## English

**Title (26):** Image to Text: OCR Scanner

**Short description (78/80):**
Free image to text scanner, no ads. Copy, translate offline, call, save notes.

**Full description (1982/4000):**

Turn any photo, screenshot or selected text into something you can act on, in one tap.

Image to Text scans text from your camera, gallery or latest screenshot, then shows exactly what you can do with it: copy it, call a number, email an address, open a link, translate it or save it as a note.

📷 SCAN TEXT FROM IMAGES
• Camera, gallery or screenshot: pick the source that suits you
• Crop to the part you need
• Text recognition runs on your device
• Latin, Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Devanagari scripts
• Fix broken lines and clean up formatting before you copy

⚡ SMART ACTIONS FOR WHAT'S IN THE TEXT
📞 Phone numbers: call, send SMS, save to contacts
✉️ Emails: write an email in one tap
🔗 Links: open the page
📍 Addresses: open in maps and get directions
📅 Dates and times: add a calendar event or reminder
🤖 Ask your favorite AI app (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and more, if installed)

🌐 TRANSLATE OFFLINE
• Translate between dozens of languages on your device
• Download a language once, then translate without internet
• You choose which language models to download, nothing is downloaded behind your back
• Reopen a past translation instantly

🕘 HISTORY
• Every action is saved, searchable and filterable
• Pin the items you use most

📝 NOTES
• Save text as notes with categories
• Pin them, or lock them with your fingerprint or screen lock
• Back up and restore your notes

📱 WORKS WHERE YOU ARE
• Select text in any app and choose "Image to Text" from the menu
• Share an image or text to the app
• Scan or create QR codes
• Read text aloud
• Home screen widget and quick settings tile
• Dark and light themes, English and Vietnamese

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Text recognition and translation run on your device
• No account needed
• The internet is only used to download language models you ask for, and when you open a link or another app yourself

🆓 FREE, FOREVER
Image to Text is free to use, with no ads and no account.
